Feeling anxious is a normal part of life. Many people worry about things such as health, money, school, work, or family. Anxiety disorders differ from everyday worry: the anxiety does not go away, is felt in many situations, and can get worse over time.

Common types include generalized anxiety disorder, panic disorder, social anxiety disorder, and phobia-related disorders.

Symptoms can significantly disrupt functioning — in severe cases people may feel intense fear in common situations, avoid social encounters, or refuse to leave their home; symptoms interfere with job performance, schoolwork, and relationships.
