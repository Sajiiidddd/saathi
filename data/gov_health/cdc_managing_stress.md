Stress is a normal part of life, but chronic stress can worsen health problems. Managing stress daily can prevent long-term stress from developing.

How stress shows up: emotional responses such as fear, anger, sadness, worry, numbness, or frustration; sleep disruption and concentration difficulties; physical symptoms such as headaches, body pain, stomach issues, and skin reactions; worsening of existing conditions; increased substance use.

Healthy coping — mind: reduce news and social-media consumption; practise relaxation (deep breathing, stretching, meditation, journaling); spend time outdoors; practise gratitude daily; connect with trusted people and community organisations.

Healthy coping — body: sleep 7+ hours nightly on a consistent schedule; build toward about two and a half hours of exercise a week in small increments; eat nutritious food and limit unhealthy fats, salt, and added sugars; limit alcohol; avoid drugs and tobacco; keep regular health appointments.
