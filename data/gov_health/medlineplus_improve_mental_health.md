Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how we think, feel, and act as we cope with life. It determines how we manage stress, relate to others, and make choices, at every stage of life.

Good mental health helps you cope with stress, be physically healthy, have good relationships, make meaningful contributions to your community, work productively, and realise your full potential.

Ways to improve mental health:
- Staying positive: find balance between positive and negative emotions; hold on to good feelings; limit exposure to upsetting news and social media.
- Practising gratitude: be thankful daily, through reflection or journaling, for good things big and small.
- Physical health: be physically active (exercise reduces stress and depression), get enough quality sleep on a regular schedule, eat healthily.
- Connecting with others: build strong relationships with family, friends, and community — volunteering and hobby groups help.
- Meaning and purpose: through work, volunteering, learning, or spirituality.
- Coping skills: learn methods to handle stressful situations flexibly and persistently.
- Meditation: practise focus and awareness in a quiet location, with a comfortable position and an open attitude toward passing thoughts.
- Relaxation techniques: progressive relaxation, guided imagery, biofeedback, self-hypnosis, and deep breathing trigger the body's natural relaxation response.
