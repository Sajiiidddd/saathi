This calming breathing technique for stress, anxiety and panic takes just a few minutes and can be done anywhere. You can do it standing up, sitting in a chair that supports your back, or lying on a bed or yoga mat. Make yourself comfortable and wear loose clothing if you can. Place your feet roughly hip-width apart.

The exercise:
1. Let your breath flow as deep down into your belly as is comfortable, without forcing it.
2. Try breathing in through your nose and out through your mouth.
3. Breathe in gently and regularly. Some people find it helpful to count steadily from 1 to 5.
4. Then let it flow out gently, counting from 1 to 5 again, if you find this helpful.
5. Repeat for at least 5 minutes.

You will get the most benefit if you do it regularly, as part of your daily routine.
