Stress can be defined as a state of worry or mental tension caused by a difficult situation. Stress is a natural response that helps us address challenges — everyone experiences it, though responses vary from person to person.

Moderate stress supports daily functioning, but excessive stress creates physical and mental health problems. Signs include difficulty relaxing, anxiety, irritability, poor concentration, headaches, body pain, digestive upset, sleep disruption, and appetite changes. Prolonged symptoms may develop into conditions that deserve professional care.

Stress management strategies:
- Keep a consistent daily routine with scheduled meals, family time, exercise, and recreation.
- Prioritise sleep: consistent schedule, restful environment, limited device use before bed.
- Stay connected with trusted people.
- Eat balanced, regular meals with adequate hydration.
- Exercise daily — a walk counts.
- Limit news consumption when it increases stress.
