Six tips for better sleep:
1. Keep a regular sleep routine. Going to bed and getting up at similar times each day helps your body know when it is time to sleep — even on days off.
2. Relax before bed. Avoid phones, tablets and computers for an hour before sleep (blue light). Try reading, meditation, breathing exercises, or calming music or podcasts.
3. Manage worries and stress. Talking to someone you trust or writing down your worries can help you manage them. A to-do list before sleep can help clear your mind.
4. Make your bedroom comfortable: quiet, dark and cool. Earplugs, silent phone mode, closed curtains, an eye mask, or gentle background sounds (rainfall, white noise) can help.
5. Try not to force sleep. If you are still awake after around 20 minutes, get up and sit somewhere comfortable; do something relaxing like gentle yoga, reading or quiet music, then return to bed.
6. Get active during the day — regular physical activity improves sleep quality; exercise earlier if evening activity disrupts your rest.

Food and drink: avoid large meals close to bedtime; limit caffeine — try warm milk or herbal tea instead; avoid alcohol and nicotine 1–2 hours before bed.
